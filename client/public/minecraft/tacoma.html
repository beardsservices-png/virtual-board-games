      How to read the plan

      • One square = one blockColumns run front to back (col 1 is the bull bar). Rows run side to side (row 1 is the passenger side, last row the driver side). Thick lines every 10.
      • Mark the footprint firstLay a 148 × 52 outline on the ground with any throwaway block, then build layer 1 inside it and break the outline after.
      • Faint squares are the layer belowUse them to line up each new layer. The amber frame in the 3D view shows the layer you're on.
      • Skip hidden blocks is onThe body is built as a 1–2 block shell, so you never fill the inside of the hood or doors. Turn it off to see a solid fill instead.
      • Row by rowUnder the grid, each row is written out as runs, e.g. "cols 12–40 B×29" means 29 Black Concrete in a straight line. Easier to follow on a TV than counting squares.
      • Glass sits flushUse full stained-glass blocks, not panes, so the sloped windshield reads correctly.
